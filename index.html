        Who are we

        Dolphin Drive are a Hamilton-based rock band made up of Brogan King on guitar and vocals and Cullen Douch on drums. Drawing influence from the best of Kiwi rock, grunge and punk, the duo have built a sound far bigger than their two-piece lineup would suggest.

        Since forming, Dolphin Drive have set their sights on taking their music beyond the Waikato, combining powerful songwriting with a no-nonsense approach to live rock music. With original material in the works and a growing presence on the local scene, the band are focused on one thing – making music that hits hard and leaves a lasting impression.

        As they continue to write, perform and push forward, Dolphin Drive are determined to establish themselves as one of the next names to watch in New Zealand rock.
